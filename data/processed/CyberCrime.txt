Information sought and background of the case: 
The Appellant filed an RTI application dated 22.04.2024 seeking information 
on following points:- 
 
“In continuation to earlier RTI application No: MHOME/R/E/22/03219 dated 
28/08/2022 and decisions thereof, following information may kindly made 
available to me for filing PIL and appeal to Honourable President of India. It is 
again reiterated that case falls under Protection of Life and Personal Liberty 
and is covered under Article 21 of the Constitution of India. 
Information Sought is as follows: 
1. A copy of complete Detailed Project Report on the bases of which the Indian 
Cybercrime Coordination Centre 14C scheme was approved by Government of 
India on 05th October 2018.” 
 
The CPIO, Director vide letter dated 21.05.2024 replied as under:- 
 
“I am to refer to your request for Information under RTI Act 2005, vide letter 
dated 22.04.2024 and received in the office of this CPIO on 22.04.2024 and to 
inform that 'Police' and 'Public Order' are State subjects as per the Seventh 
Schedule of the Constitution of India and States/UTs are primarily responsible 
for prevention, detection and investigation of such crime through their law 
Enforcement Agencies (LEAs). 
2. The Ministry of Home Affairs, Government of India has set up the Indian 
Cybercrime Coordination Centre (14C) to deal with all type of Cybercrime in a 
coordinated and comprehensive manner in the country in the year 2018.
