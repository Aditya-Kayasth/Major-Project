ORAL ORDER 
1.  Petitioner is before this Court seeking for the fol lowing 
reliefs: 
a.  Issue a writ in the nature of certiorari quashing t he 
impugned memorandum bearing 
No.020/1.5/158/Misc./2025/DLS 11.04.2025 issued to 
the Petitioner by the 2nd Respondent at Annexure 'J ' to 
the Writ Petition, by allowing this Writ Petition; 
 
b.  Grant costs; and 
 
c.  Such other reliefs as this Hon'ble Court deems fit to 
grant in the facts and circumstances of the case an d in 
the interest of justice and equity. 
 
2.  A memo for withdrawal dated 14.01.2026 has been fil ed 
signed by the petitioner and the counsel for the 
petitioner, which reads as under:  
"The Petitioner submits that the present writ petit ion is 
filed to challenging the impugned memorandum dated 
14.03.2025 issued to the petitioner by the 2 nd  respondent at 
Annexure-J to the writ petition.  The petitioner is  no more a 
member of the Board of the Space Employees' Housing  Co-
operative Society Ltd. Hence, the present writ peti tion may 
be dismissed as not pressed.  The present memo may be 
taken on record, in the interest of justice and equity."  
 
3.  Accepting the said memo and the submission of the 
counsel for the petitioner, the petition is dismiss ed as 
withdrawn.
