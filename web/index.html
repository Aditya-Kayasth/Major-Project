Five-stage legal retrieval funnel

  1. Statutory concordance expansion. Queries citing CrPC / IPC / Evidence Act sections are expanded with their BNSS / BNS / BSA equivalents (and vice versa), bridging the July 2024 criminal-law transition.
  2. Dual candidate generation. Dense semantic search (local embedding model, cosine, ChromaDB) and BM25 lexical search each return their top 60 child chunks. BM25 catches exact statutory tokens ("Section 138", "Article 21") that embeddings blur.
  3. Reciprocal Rank Fusion. RRF (k = 60) merges the two rankings using ranks only, so incompatible score scales never need calibration. The top 30 fused candidates move on.
  4. Cross-encoder reranking. bge-reranker-v2-m3 reads query and passage jointly. A bounded Stare Decisis prior (court tier, bench strength, ratio vs. obiter; at most ±5%) breaks ties between equally relevant passages without overriding relevance.
  5. Parent expansion & grounded synthesis. Each ~400-character child chunk maps to its ~1,500-character parent section in SQLite; parents are passed to a large LLM (Llama 3.3 70B via an OpenAI-compatible API, with Gemini as fallback) with a zero-hallucination instruction to cite only retrieved precedent.

Legal-aware chunking

Parent sections are flushed at rhetorical transitions ("Learned counsel submitted…" vs. "We hold that…") so counsel's arguments and the court's ratio never share a chunk. Each child chunk is tagged with its rhetorical role and case title.

Engineering under constraints

Shared GPU slice with RAM/VRAM/disk guardrails, resumable checkpointed indexing with deterministic chunk IDs, embedded SQLite (no daemons), and an LLM router with provider fallback, rate limiting and a response cache.
